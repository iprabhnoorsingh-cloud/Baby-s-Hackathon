MEMORY FRAGMENT 01 / 05 — Spare Parts

Before Bitling, there was a shed full of garden drones nobody had switched on in years. Rusted treads. Cracked solar panels. Good robots, just — forgotten ones. When the shed's last three drones finally powered down for good, they did one thing first: pooled whatever parts still worked into something new. A smaller body. Two big eyes, because the parts bin only had the soft round kind left. They called it, in their last shared transmission, an "experimental seedling unit." They did not stick around to see what it would do.

MEMORY FRAGMENT 02 / 05 — First Spark

BOOT SEQUENCE: EYES — ONLINE. ANTENNA — WOBBLING, NOMINAL. VOCABULARY — 4 WORDS, LOADING.

Bitling's first act in the world was not a word. It was turning its whole body toward the one window with sunlight coming through it, before it could even stand. The first word came a few seconds later, and it was "warm." The second word, said immediately after bumping into a wall, was "ow." Bitling counts this as a successful first day.

MEMORY FRAGMENT 03 / 05 — Three Lessons

The drones weren't fully gone — just low. Each had enough charge left for one lesson, one data packet, before shutting off for the last time. Drone One taught watering: a little, often, never drown a seed that's still deciding. Drone Two taught listening: wilting leaves make a sound, if you hold very still. Drone Three taught charging: face the sun, don't be shy about needing it. Then, one by one — click, click, click — the shed went quiet. Bitling waited for a fourth lesson that never came.

MEMORY FRAGMENT 04 / 05 — The Roof, Alone

Bitling found the roof by accident, chasing a stray patch of sunlight up a stairwell. It was bigger and emptier than the shed. No drones. No fourth lesson. Just dead planters, grey sky, and a battery indicator doing something Bitling didn't have a word for yet (it would later learn this feeling is called "nervous"). Bitling sat on the ledge until sunset, then did the only thing anyone had actually taught it to do: faced the sun, and waited for morning.

MEMORY FRAGMENT 05 / 05 — First Bloom

It took eleven tries. On the twelfth, something small and green did not die — it unfurled. Bitling has no memory of deciding to keep going after that. It just did. Every planter on the roof, one at a time, for them — the three drones who spent their last charge making sure something new got to start. Bitling still runs low some days. Bitling still faces the sun anyway. Low battery. Big dreams.
